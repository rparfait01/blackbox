What this does and does not tell you

It tells you whether the evidence section of the report is byte-for-byte what BLACK BOX signed, and when it was signed.

It does not tell you that any particular thing happened. BLACK BOX does not interpret, transcribe, or assess a recording — it attests to the record's structure and integrity. The survivor's statement section is deliberately not covered by the signature: it is her own account, and she can revise it at any time without affecting the certification.
