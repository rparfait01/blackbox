RHEZIA

ray-ZEE-ah  ·  n.  ·  from Gk. παρρησία

Parrhesia — the ancient right to speak the truth without fear, even to the person who holds power over you. Rhezia is that right, made operational, for people whose truth is dangerous to say out loud.

One

The palette

Three colors carry the brand. A fourth set governs the app, and they are deliberately different — the app is dark because it runs at night, on a low battery, in a pocket.

Brand

Midnight Ink #071A2B Primary. Type, the mark, dark surfaces. Never pure black in brand contexts — the blue keeps it human.
Archival Brass #C79A42 The record. Marks a terminus, a seal, a thing kept. Never used for warnings.
Warm Parchment #F5F1E8 Ground. Paper, not screen. Everything the public sees sits on this.

Product

True Black #000000 App ground. OLED-dark for battery and for a screen that does not light a room.
Signal Amber #e89a00 The record, in-product. Capture, custody, evidence state.
Alert Red #D6453C Warnings only. Separated from amber so a warning never reads as a record and a record never reads as a warning.
Stillpoint Teal #4fd1a5 Facade only. Belongs to the covert presentation and must never appear in Rhezia brand material.

Amber and red were one color. They are now two. The record is a comfort; a warning is not. Nothing should have to carry both meanings.

Two

The mark

One continuous stroke. It starts, it travels, and it stops at a brass square. The stroke is the account; the square is the moment it becomes permanent.

Primary
Reverse
One color
  • Clearspace on all sides equals the height of the brass square.
  • Minimum size 16px. Verified legible at that size; below it, use the icon.
  • One color is a supported lockup, not a fallback. The mark reads as R without the brass.
  • App icon is the reverse mark on Midnight Ink, 22.5% corner radius.
  • Never add a ring, shield, lock, keyhole, or crosshair. Every safety product uses those and they all say fear.
  • Never recolor the brass square to red. It marks a record, not an alarm.
  • Never outline, emboss, gradient, or rotate the mark.
  • Never place the mark on the Stillpoint facade, in any form, at any size.

Four

Voice

Plain. Certain. Never dramatic — the situation supplies the drama. State the facts once and let her decide.

Say

Your recording may not be saved.

Not

Warning! Recording failure detected!

Say

No capture has reached us. Do not read silence as calm.

Not

No activity detected.

Say

These recordings will be permanently deleted.

Not

Are you absolutely sure? This cannot be undone!

Say

2 of 2 contacts were notified before you took coordination.

Not

All contacts successfully alerted!

  • Never victim. Never panic.
  • Never survivor in the interface — that is a word she chooses, not one we assign.
  • Never congratulate her for being brave. Never ask if she is sure twice.
  • Never state a status the system has not observed.

Six

Site copy

rhezia.com. Three pages. The app entry point is a separate origin and is never linked from here.

Home · hero

She should not have to be the witness.

Rhezia records what happens, keeps it in a form a court can verify, and gets a real person moving — in the order most likely to reach her fastest.

Home · the sequence

Her support contact first. The person who knows her, knows the history, and can act without needing the situation explained.

Then the next contact. Ten seconds apart, until someone takes coordination. The moment someone does, the rest are never alarmed.

Then 911. Last, because it starts from zero. Not because it matters less.

In an emergency, deciding who is doing what is not a job she has the capacity for. It should not be hers even a little.

Home · the record

Encrypted before it leaves her phone. We hold ciphertext, a hash, and wrapped keys. We hold no key that opens any of it.

Verifiable by someone who does not trust us. The verification tool is standalone and open. A record that vouches for itself proves nothing.

Hers to destroy. If a recording on her phone is dangerous to her, she deletes it. The signed proof that it existed — and that its destruction was hers — survives. Nobody gets to argue it never happened.

For organizations

Survivors never pay.

Shelters and coalitions get Rhezia at no cost. Grant-funded institutions license it in tiers. Individuals pay once.

Per-organization data inventory, export, and deletion on termination. Reporting output that fits VAWA, VOCA, and FVPSA without an advocate rebuilding it by hand every quarter.
